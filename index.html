Common questions

Quick answers. Call us if yours isn't here.

What is a Dura-Seal Certified Care Centre?

We are an approved applicator of Dura-Seal protection. That means we do new and used vehicle applications and attend to Dura-Seal warranty work.

Can I buy Dura-Seal products without booking a detail?

Yes. Retail and wholesale Dura-Seal products are stocked in store at 4 Te Puia Drive, Aotea.

Which vehicles do you work on?

All makes and models of cars, RVs and caravans, marine and watercraft, and motorcycles.

Do you work with car dealers?

Yes. We apply protection to new and used vehicles for dealers and offer wholesale product pricing.
